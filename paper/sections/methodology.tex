\section{Benchmark and Evaluation Methodology}
\label{sec:method}
DiagBench uses deterministic generators to make fictional records. Each
incident separates oracle data from the question, rows, and logs in the
ModelView. Visible evidence IDs are stable. Construction audits test the
intended relation and known shortcuts. They do not prove realism or
difficulty.

The experiments use frozen development sets. Incident 001 uses seeds
1000--1004. Incident 002 uses seeds 1000, 1001, 1003, 1004, 1005, and 1017,
with three logical trials. Incident 003 v3 uses constructions 1319, 1299,
1387, and 1222. Each has target and temporal-twin queries over the same
evidence. The paper has no final held-out evaluation.

The prompt requires five RCA labels, an explanation, and evidence IDs.
Incidents 001 and 002 use frozen v2 prompt, taxonomy, and scorer versions.
Incident 003 uses v3. Provider calls use medium reasoning and omit
temperature. The records include hashes and version data. Incorrect answers
are not retried. Transport failures are missing responses, not wrong RCAs.

support, and representation exposure. These are separate outcomes. The three

rebuild aggregates but cannot inspect the exact historical responses.
