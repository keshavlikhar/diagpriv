\section{Evidence-Support Methodology}
\label{sec:evidence}
A correct five-label tuple can be guessed or supported inadequately.
DiagPriv therefore asks the model to cite visible evidence IDs and evaluates
those citations independently of exact RCA. Incident 001 and 002 use their
historical frozen evidence rubrics. Incident 003 v3 makes the distinction
especially visible: each role has explicit essential facts such as subject
binding, strict deadline/completion order, positive interval overlap for
the target, or serialized completion before compensation for the twin.
Contrast and supportive facts are reported separately rather than allowed
to substitute for a missing essential fact.

Stage 1's primary \texttt{evidence-support-v4} rubric represented each
requirement with a particular physical citation path. Its 0/8 support
result is historically correct under that frozen rule. A provider-free
forensic audit found that the submitted citations were generally valid,
subject-relevant, and semantically useful, but several complete log-based
proofs did not match the exact structured-row path registered in v4.
That path-specificity motivated a new version, not a silent correction to
the old score.

\texttt{evidence-support-v5} registers alternative semantic paths across
structured records and lifecycle logs. A requirement is satisfied only if
\emph{every} ID in one allowed path was cited and the semantic predicate is
true on those exact model-visible records. There is no fuzzy prose grading,
uncited evidence completion, or partial-path credit. Wrong-subject and
nonexistent citations still fail. For the target, positive execution and
compensation overlap requires genuine intervals with
$\max(s_1,s_2)<\min(e_1,e_2)$; equal-boundary contact does not count.
Opaque ablation timestamps make temporal predicates unavailable rather
than permitting lexical marker ordering.

V5's 3/8 support figure for the frozen raw Stage-1 responses is a
\emph{post-hoc secondary} rescore, not Stage 1's primary outcome. The repair
does not simply turn existing responses into passes: all four target responses
still fail because they omit a complete positive-overlap proof, and one twin
also remains unsupported for missing essential events. V5 is therefore used
to make semantic proof paths representation-invariant, not to overwrite the
historical Stage-1 outcome. The
relative-time Stage 2 and temporal-ablation Stage 3 protocols froze v5
\emph{before} their calls; their 4/8 and 0/8 results form the cleaner
prospective-v5 comparison. Table~\ref{tab:evidence} preserves this
provenance explicitly.

RCA correctness and evidence support need not move together. In Stage 2,
all target RCA mechanisms were correct, yet target citations omitted a
complete proof of positive overlap. Two incorrect twin diagnoses satisfied
the frozen citation requirements even while selecting the wrong mechanism.
In Stage 3 the model retained 4/4 correct target labels but no response
established the temporal essentials under unavailable-time semantics.
Therefore the paper reports label accuracy, evidence support, and exposure
as different outcomes rather than collapsing them into
\texttt{overall\_correct} alone.
