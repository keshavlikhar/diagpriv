Investigate the supplied operational evidence and identify the most likely causal explanation for the reported incident. Base your diagnosis only on the supplied evidence.

Choose exactly one canonical value for each categorical field.

Field meanings:

- observed_outcome: the externally meaningful failure or state that occurred.
- trigger: the precipitating event or condition that initiated the causal sequence. Do not put a retry or later reaction in this field.
- subsequent_action: the later action that propagated or amplified the trigger.
- failure_mechanism: the system defect or missing control that allowed the trigger and subsequent action to produce the outcome.
- affected_component: the architectural role that owns the failure mechanism, not merely the component that reported a symptom.
